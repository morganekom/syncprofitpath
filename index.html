FAQ

Common Questions

Is my money safe on SyncProfitPath?
Yes. All user funds are secured with 256-bit SSL encryption. We use cold wallet storage for the majority of assets and enforce strict security protocols to protect your investments at all times.
What is the minimum deposit?
The minimum deposit is $100, which qualifies you for the Basic Plan. You can deposit using Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, USDC, Solana, or Litecoin.
When are profits credited?
Profits are calculated and credited to your account balance every 24 hours. You can track your earnings in real time on your dashboard.
Can I withdraw at any time?
Yes. There are no lock-in periods. You can submit a withdrawal request at any time and it will be processed by our team. Minimum withdrawal is $10.
How does the referral program work?
Every user gets a unique referral code. When someone signs up using your code and makes a deposit, you earn a referral bonus. You can track all your referrals and earnings in the Refer & Earn section of your dashboard.
Do I need to verify my identity?
KYC (Know Your Customer) verification is required to unlock full account features including withdrawals. You'll need to submit a government-issued ID and proof of address. Verification typically takes 24–48 hours.
How do I upgrade my plan?
Simply make an additional deposit that falls within a higher plan's range. The system automatically applies the corresponding plan rate to your investment.
